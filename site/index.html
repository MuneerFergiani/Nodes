A MINUTE TO LEARN

Everything is
connected.

  1. Make your move.You are teal. Drag through several friendly nodes and release on a target to send the configured share from each (50% by default). Each drag starts a fresh selection. Releasing sends the order and clears the selection; releasing on empty space cancels it. Single taps do not select nodes.
  2. Grow your corner.Your nodes produce units. Outnumber a neutral or enemy node to capture it. Move through your own nodes to reach further.
  3. Build something bigger.Double-tap a friendly node to upgrade. An upward-chevron badge means it is ready. Tiers change shape: circle, square, hexagon, diamond. Higher tiers grow faster and hold more units.
