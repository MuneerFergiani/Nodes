Privacy policy.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Nodes is a strategy game. For privacy questions or requests about your data, contact info@AlchemyEvolved.com.

Playing and signing in

You can play the tutorial, single player, quick play, and custom games without creating an account. Ranked play requires a Google sign-in.

When you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your account identifier, email address, name, and profile picture information to our authentication provider, Supabase. Nodes uses this information to create and recognise your account, keep you signed in, and confirm eligibility for ranked play. Nodes does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or contacts, and never receives your Google password.

Multiplayer and ranked records

The game server processes your display name, lobby membership, gameplay commands, connection information, and match state to run multiplayer games. Other players can see your chosen display name, lobby information, and the shared game state. Your sign-in email is not included in the public lobby listing.

Ranked records include account identifiers, ratings, games played, wins, match identifiers, participants, outcomes, ruleset, and completion time. These records support rankings and prevent results being counted twice. Clients cannot write ranked results directly; signed-in players can read their own ranked records.

Browser storage

Your browser stores game preferences and configurable game rules locally. Supabase stores the sign-in session in browser storage so you remain signed in. You can sign out and clear this site's browser storage; clearing local storage does not delete server-side account or ranked records.

Service providers and technical data

GitHub Pages hosts the website, Colyseus Cloud runs multiplayer servers, and Supabase handles authentication and ranked storage. Google handles Google sign-in and serves some game fonts. These providers receive technical information needed to deliver their services, such as IP addresses, request details, and browser information. Provider processing may take place in countries other than your own.

Nodes uses server-side multiplayer match summaries to understand game balance and connection reliability. Summaries contain match identifiers, mode, map size and speed, player counts, signed-in player counts, start/end times, duration, winning team number, end reason, disconnect count, and forfeits. They do not contain player names, email addresses, account IDs, IP addresses, or raw gameplay inputs. Summaries are stored in Supabase and are not readable by game clients. Match identifiers can be associated with ranked records by the operator. For playtesting, Nodes also sends lightweight browser events for menu sessions, game starts and finishes, visible play activity, and tutorial lesson completion. Events include times, mode, duration, outcome, lesson, build version, and a broad touch or mouse input class. A random identifier in browser storage recognises returning guest browsers; a hashed account identifier recognises signed-in players. These pseudonymous identifiers do not include names or emails, but allow activity to be linked over time. Guest and account activity are not merged. Only approved dashboard administrators can read aggregated reports. You can stop future browser event collection using Playtest analytics in Settings. Clearing browser storage resets your guest identifier. No advertising integration is used. Hosting and authentication providers may maintain operational and security logs. Nodes does not sell Google account data or use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

Account and ranked records are kept to provide your account and match history; there is currently no automatic age-based deletion schedule. Temporary retry files may hold ranked results until they are saved successfully. Analytics summaries older than 90 days are removed when new summaries are received; on an inactive service they remain until the next ingestion. Playtest events older than 90 days and pseudonymous player records inactive for more than 90 days are removed on new event ingestion. First-play dates are retained while the player record remains active. On an inactive service these records remain until ingestion resumes. Disabling collection does not delete previously saved events. Provider logs and backups have their own retention periods.

Use the support contact to request access, correction, or deletion of your account information and associated ranked records. We may ask you to verify account ownership. You can also remove Nodes' access in your Google Account connection settings; this stops future Google access but does not itself delete existing Nodes records.
